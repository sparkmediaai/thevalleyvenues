Where everyone sleeps.

Four cottages along the hill, a lodge for the largest party, and one cottage in the woods for the two of you. Thirty-four beds, a minute from the end of the evening.

The village

Four cottages, each turned to face out.

Phoenix, Bluebird, Goldfinch and Hummingbird sit along the hill above the hall, each one turned toward the view rather than toward the next, so nobody is looking into anybody else’s morning. Three sleep four and one sleeps six — a queen bed and a queen pull-out in each — with a kitchenette inside and a shared outdoor kitchen between them: a grill, a pizza oven, and a hammock for whoever is done.

Private hot tubs are coming. Between Saturdays the village is open as a stay on its own terms.

  • CottagesFour. Phoenix, Bluebird, Goldfinch, Hummingbird.
  • SleepsEighteen across the village. Three cottages sleep four, one sleeps six.
  • InsideA queen bed, a queen pull-out, a kitchenette with a stovetop, fridge, microwave and dishwasher.
  • Between themAn outdoor kitchen with a grill and a pizza oven. A hammock. Hot tubs on the way.
  • SitsThree hundred and twenty-five feet up the hill from Davis Hall, on foot.
The Lodge

For the largest party, and the morning.

The Lodge takes the wedding party or the biggest family group, and it has two rooms that matter on the day. The Willow Suite is a private hair and makeup studio, so the morning starts where you slept and not in a car. The Mahogany Suite is a pool table, a card table and a seventy-inch screen, for whoever is still up at one.

And then

Lost in the Woods.

One cottage, tucked away at the far edge of the property, for the two of you. A king bed. A full kitchen. A walk-in shower and a soaking tub. Outside, your own kitchen and grill, a fire pit sunk into the ground, and a fountain you will hear before you see. It is where the wedding party gathers the night before, and where the two of you disappear to afterward — married, alone, and thirty seconds from everyone you love.

  • Lost in the WoodsOne cottage. A king bed, a full kitchen, a walk-in shower and a soaking tub.
  • OutsideYour own kitchen and grill, an in-ground fire pit, a fountain.
  • In allThirty-four beds on the estate. A hotel is six minutes away for everyone else.

Where the direction and the live site differ. The live site sells Lost in the Woods as a bookable honeymoon suite. The direction document folds it into the Estate Weekend as the emotional close rather than offering it separately, and this page follows the direction. Bachelorette weekends are also sold on the live site; the framework retires the word “package” in guest copy, so if they stay, they are a stay.
