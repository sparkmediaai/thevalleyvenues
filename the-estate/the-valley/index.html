What it is

Held on three sides, open on the fourth.

The Valley is the flat, open ground through the middle of the property, and the hills around it are why it works. They keep the sound in and the wind out, and they hold the last half hour of light after it has left the grass. The fourth side is the mountain.

This is where you marry. It is also where the rehearsal happens the night before, where cocktails can be poured under the sky, and where dinner can be laid if the evening is the kind that wants to stay outside. Whatever is set here is set for one couple: there is no second ceremony on the property, and nobody’s arch comes down while yours goes up.

It is eight hundred and sixty feet from the front door of Magnolia House, and everyone walks it.

  • HoldsThe ceremony. The rehearsal. Cocktails and dinner under the sky when the evening allows.
  • FacesLookout Mountain, across the open side.
  • Held byRidgeline on three sides. Sound stays in, wind stays out.
  • SeatsUp to three hundred, in the meadow, facing the mountain.
  • If it rainsThe conservatory at Magnolia House. No tent, no flip fee, no five o’clock decision.
  • ThenA thousand feet, on foot, up to the deck — the longest walk of the weekend.
