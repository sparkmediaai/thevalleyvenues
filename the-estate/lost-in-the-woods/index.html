Lost in the Woods.

One cabin at the far edge of the property, with a fire pit, a terrace and a kitchen of its own. Small enough for the two of you, and enough of a place to gather in.

What it is

A cabin at the far edge, and a place in its own right.

Lost in the Woods sits away from everything else on the property, down the drive past the meadow, where the trees close in. Inside: a king bed, a full kitchen with a pull-out couch, and a walk-in shower and soaking tub under the timber. Outside is the part people do not expect — an outdoor kitchen and grill of its own, a fire pit sunk into the stone, a fountain you hear before you see it, and a terrace strung with lights over the valley.

That makes it more than a bed. It is where the wedding party gathers the night before, where a small ceremony can be held at the fire pit with the ridge behind it, and where the two of you disappear to afterward — married, alone, and thirty seconds from everyone you love.
