What it is

The house the wedding party wakes up in.

The Lodge stands on the drive between Overlook Village and Davis Hall, and it takes the wedding party or the biggest family group in one building rather than splitting them across cottages. It is the largest of the three places to sleep on the estate, and it is a two-minute walk down the hill to the hall and back up again at the end of the night.

Two rooms in it matter on the day itself. The Willow Suite is a private hair and makeup studio — twin mirrors, a double vanity, and the light to work in — so the morning starts where you slept, with everyone in one place, and not in a car at eight. The Mahogany Suite is the other end of the same day: a pool table, a card table and a seventy-inch screen, for whoever is still up at one and does not want to be quiet yet.

Between Saturdays the Lodge is open as a stay on its own terms, like the rest of the village.
