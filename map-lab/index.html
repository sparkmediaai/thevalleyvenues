Ten maps

Ten treatments of the same estate, for choosing between rather than for shipping. Every one of them hovers to a real photograph of the real place.

Nothing on the site links here and every page carries noindex. The map currently live on /the-estate/ is closest to 01.

01

Storybook Plate

The drawing as a printed plate: paper ground, a ruled border, a breeze through the woodland, and a building that lifts when you reach for it.

02

Golden Hour

The same ground in daylight and again after dark, cross-faded on a slow clock. Drag the hour if you would rather choose it yourself.

03

The Weekend

A marker walks the estate in the order a wedding meets it, pausing where the weekend pauses. Scrub it, or let it run.

04

The Model

The plate tipped back into three dimensions, the way a scale model sits on a table, with the buildings standing up off it. Move the pointer to walk around it.

05

Medallions

No illustration at all. The drives stay, the woodland goes, and each place is the photograph of itself. The quietest of the ten, and the most editorial.
