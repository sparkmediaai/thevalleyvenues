Ten maps

Ten treatments of the same estate, for choosing between rather than for shipping. Every one of them hovers to a real photograph of the real place.

Nothing on the site links here and every page carries noindex. The map currently live on /the-estate/ is closest to 01.

01

Storybook Plate

The drawing as a printed plate: paper ground, a ruled border, a breeze through the woodland, and a building that lifts when you reach for it.

02

Golden Hour

The same ground in daylight and again after dark, cross-faded on a slow clock. Drag the hour if you would rather choose it yourself.

03

The Weekend

A marker walks the estate in the order a wedding meets it, pausing where the weekend pauses. Scrub it, or let it run.

04

The Model

The plate tipped back the way a scale model sits on a table, lit from above and casting a shadow. Move the pointer to walk around it.

05

Medallions

No illustration at all. The drives stay, the woodland goes, and each place is the photograph of itself. The quietest of the ten, and the most editorial.

06

Depth

A diorama rather than a drawing. Mountain, far woodland, near woodland and buildings each sit on their own plane and move at their own rate.

07

Four Seasons

One drawing, four times of year. The woodland and the ground change and nothing else does, which is the point: the estate sells a different day each season.

08

The Fold

A paper map, folded in three and opening as you arrive. The creases stay, because a map that has been in somebody's pocket is a map that was used.

09

The Tour

It shows itself. The frame travels the estate on its own, resting on each place long enough to look at it, and stops the moment you take hold of it.

10

Endpaper

The inside cover of the book. No roads and no woodland -- six framed photographs on bare ground, joined by the path a weekend takes between them.
