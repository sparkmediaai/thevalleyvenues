Lidar Terraces

The same map on the real ground. The hills are the county's LiDAR survey of these 74 acres, contoured and softened into terraces; the map rises out of the valley floor one level at a time before anything is built on it. The survey is warped onto Kobi's plan, which is drawn to be read, not to scale -- so the terraces are true in shape and approximate in place.
