How One Couple’s Chattanooga Wedding Venue Connected Past and Present Generations

Looking for a Chattanooga wedding venue? Choose The Valley Venues for its southern charm, beautiful views, and family legacy.

One of the most crucial parts of any wedding is where you decide to tie the knot. With an abundance of locations nationwide, each venue style offers something unique. No matter where you say “I do,” your venue should symbolize your love. For one couple, their outdoor wedding venue honored generations of family history.

Ally and Arden were married at The Valley Venues near Chattanooga, Tennessee, on a beautiful spring day. The Valley Venues was overjoyed to host their big day, as Ally’s grandfather owned the estate before it was purchased by the current co-owner, Kobi Cummings. This special couple’s wedding tied in the rich lore of the estate’s grounds, touching the hearts of The Valley Venue’s staff and beyond. 

Ally and Arden’s Big Day

Kobi met Ally long before planning her wedding day. They had an immediate connection after meeting at a bridal show, where they discovered how similar their upbringings were. Soon after, Ally and her grandmother visited The Valley Venue’s estate to get a feel for Ally’s wedding day. Overjoyed, Kobi knew that this wedding would stand out as one of her favorite events to host. 

Ally’s aunt was also married on the property long before it was The Valley Venues. This special familial connection furthered Ally’s immediate appreciation for having her wedding on The Valley Venues estate. Ally and Arden were married amongst the gorgeous daffodils planted by her grandmother while her family owned the property, which were in full bloom for the occasion.

Kobi states, “This wedding was extra special to us. It was filled with love and history, and the sweetest walk down memory lane as the wonderful Ally and Arden united their lives in a place that holds so much meaning for them. We’re honored you chose to return here to celebrate your special day with us. Wishing you both a lifetime of love and happiness together!”

A Timeless Chattanooga Wedding Venue

Each outdoor wedding space at The Valley Venues is imbued with charm and deep-rooted history. The 74-acre estate in Wildwood, Georgia, just outside Chattanooga, provides the perfect backdrop for any wedding or event. The outdoor spaces, The Valley and the Lookout Deck, give guests a picture-perfect view of the rolling hills and lush forest enveloping the property. The daffodils planted in the valley by Ally’s grandmother tie in the perfect touch to any wedding, honoring the property’s past. 

The story of Ally and Arden and The Valley Venues is one of honoring family and fate. Kobi and The Valley Venue’s team put on Ally and Arden’s outdoor wedding without a hitch, and the couple was happily married in the green grass of The Valley. This full-circle moment perfectly encapsulates how meaningful personal touches are in weddings, even if it’s a small keepsake or a passed-down tradition. The Valley Venues is a flawless Chattanooga wedding venue that connects southern charm and family ties with the everlasting love of many.
