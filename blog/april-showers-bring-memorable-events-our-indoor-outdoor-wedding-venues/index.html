April Showers Bring Memorable Events: Our Indoor & Outdoor Wedding Venues

Our versatile indoor and outdoor wedding venues are here to set the backdrop for your spring wedding, no matter the vision you bring to us.

Spring is a season of new beginnings, bringing blooming flowers and seasonal weddings. But as with any change in seasons, we also get a shift in weather. Colder temperatures make way for milder, sunny days, but we also have spring showers to look forward to. And while April showers bring May flowers, they can make spring wedding planning a little tricky—especially when it comes to your choice of wedding ceremony sites and reception venues.

Venue choice is a key part of the wedding planning process. The choice can be difficult to make when there are considerations like weather and how much control you have over the space itself to keep in mind. And for spring weddings especially, having indoor and outdoor options to choose from for your wedding ceremony and reception venue can be beneficial, given the possibility of inclement weather.

Here at The Valley Venues, you’ll find a carefully curated selection of indoor and outdoor ceremony sites and reception options for your wedding. We’ve meticulously crafted our venue offerings to provide all our guests the experience of a lifetime—whether you’re looking to celebrate among the rolling hills or inside a gorgeous, fully restored 1890s home. 

There are many benefits to consider when it comes to our estate—let’s dig into a few together as you begin planning your spring wedding with The Valley Venues.

Our Outdoor Wedding Venues

Springtime in Wildwood, Georgia, located just outside of Chattanooga, Tennessee, is stunning—with fresh greenery and mountain views just about anywhere you look across our estate. Our two outdoor wedding venue options offer a truly unique and elegant experience that fully makes use of the natural beauty found at The Valley Venues.

The Valley

Once a beloved horse-riding arena, The Valley is our expansive green space that is now framed by vibrant flowers and lush forests. You’ll find The Valley to be a jaw-dropping spot for your spring wedding, rehearsal dinner, or reception, set against the natural forestry that surrounds this transformed space.

Lookout Deck

The Lookout Deck is decorated with romantic lighting and peers out over the mountains that surround our estate. Perfect for a stunning sunrise or sunset wedding, you’ll find modern provisions and ceremony seating for up to 250 guests in this enchanting outdoor wedding venue.   

Indoor Wedding Ceremony and Reception Venues

Mother Nature doesn’t always cooperate with our plans, which makes an indoor wedding ceremony and reception venue a fantastic choice for your memorable day—you won’t have to worry about unpredictable weather changes, and you’ll still experience a truly enchanting backdrop. No matter the ambiance you’re looking for, our two indoor venues provide gorgeous settings for all your wedding needs.

Magnolia House

Magnolia House is our hallmark building that has been lovingly restored to offer our guests a unique venue that ties romance and history together. This house provides an enchanting space for cocktail hours, receptions, and ceremonies that can be tailored to your unique vision.

Davis Hall

Perfect for any occasion, Davis Hall provides room for up to 250 attendees and a state-of-the-art sound and lighting system. Rain or shine, our grand reception hall is bright and open, making it a lovely location for your wedding reception, rehearsal dinner, or ceremony. 

Don’t Let Spring Rain on Your Parade

At The Valley Venues estate, our versatile indoor and outdoor wedding venues are here to set the backdrop for your spring wedding, no matter the vision you bring to us. You can opt for a more private, indoor setting for an enchanting ceremony or an open-air cocktail hour where you and your guests are surrounded by nature. Or, if you’re stuck between choosing one or the other, you can even custom-select a combination of indoor and outdoor venues for your special day.
Selecting the right wedding ceremony and reception venue for your special day is an important decision. And we’re here to help you create timeless, beautiful memories with our big-picture wedding planning every step of the way. To learn more about how our founder and Certified Wedding Planner, Kobi Cummings, can help you bring your dream wedding to life, contact us today!
