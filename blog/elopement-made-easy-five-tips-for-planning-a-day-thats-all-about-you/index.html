Elopement Made Easy: Five Tips for Planning a Day That’s All About You

Plan an intimate, low-key elopement at The Valley Venues. Here are some tips to help make your wedding day meaningful and exciting.

Elopements are no longer about running off to a courthouse in secret—they’ve become a deeply personal, romantic way for couples to celebrate their love on their terms. Whether you’re dreaming of exchanging vows on a scenic mountainside or keeping things simple with an intimate ceremony close to home, eloping offers an exciting and meaningful alternative to the traditional wedding. Here’s everything you need to know to plan the perfect elopement and how The Valley Venues can help you create a magical day.  

Why Couples Are Saying “Yes” to Elopements

Elopements are quickly becoming a popular choice for couples seeking a more intimate and less stressful way to celebrate their marriage.

From juggling guest lists and vendor contracts to figuring out seating arrangements, it’s easy to lose sight of what the day is really about—you and your partner. Here are some reasons why eloping has become the go-to option for many couples:

1. Less Stress, More Fun

    Traditional weddings come with a lot of moving parts, and that can be overwhelming. Eloping is a low-key option that lets you focus on each other instead of coordinating a big event. Think less about timelines and more about moments.

    2. More Intimate Moments

      For some couples, saying “I do” in front of hundreds of people isn’t appealing. Eloping allows you to have a quiet, meaningful ceremony with just the two of you (or a small group of loved ones), making it all about the connection you share.

      3. Budget-Friendly 

        Let’s face it, big weddings can be expensive. By eloping, you can save money and put those funds toward something else—like an unforgettable honeymoon or a down payment on your first home.

        4. An Adventure You’ll Never Forget

          Eloping isn’t just about getting married—it’s about doing it in a way that excites you. From a mountaintop at sunrise to a quiet garden surrounded by nature, your ceremony can take place anywhere you want. 

          The Perks of Eloping

          Besides keeping things simple, elopements offer some unique benefits that can make your wedding day even more special:

          • Ultimate Flexibility: No schedules, no timelines, no pressure. Elopements give you the freedom to design the day exactly how you want it. Want to get married at sunrise? Go for it! Want to plan a spontaneous post-ceremony road trip? The sky’s the limit.
          • Focus on the Experience: Without all the extra wedding-day obligations, eloping allows you to focus on the experience itself. You get to live in the moment, genuinely connecting with your partner. It’s less about the party and more about the meaning behind your vows.
          • The Perfect Excuse for a Celebration Later: Eloping doesn’t mean skipping out on celebrations with family and friends. Many couples who elope later plan a reception or party to share the joy with their loved ones. It’s the best of both worlds—you get your intimate ceremony and still have the option to throw a party when you’re ready.

          Five Tips for Planning the Perfect Elopement

          While eloping is simpler than a full-scale wedding, a bit of planning still goes a long way. Here are some tips to help you create a memorable elopement:

          1. Pick the Right Location.

            The beauty of eloping is the flexibility it offers. Think about places that have meaning to both of you—whether it’s the first spot you vacationed together or somewhere you’ve always dreamed of going. The Valley Venues, nestled in the scenic hills of Wildwood, Georgia, just outside of Chattanooga, Tennessee, offers stunning natural landscapes perfect for an intimate elopement, whether you’re dreaming of a rustic outdoor charm with all the modern amenities of world-class event space.

            2. Make it Legal.

              Even though eloping can feel carefree, don’t forget the legalities. Depending on where you elope, you might need a marriage license, and some locations have specific rules about who can officiate. Be sure to check local requirements beforehand to avoid any last-minute surprises.

              3. Add Personal Touches.

                One of the best parts of eloping is that you can make it 100% personal. Write your own vows, wear something non-traditional, or incorporate elements that reflect your relationship. It’s all about what feels right for you. Want to pop a bottle of champagne on a mountaintop or have a picnic after your ceremony? Do it!

                4. Capture the Moment.

                  Just because you’re eloping doesn’t mean you should skip out on documenting the day. Hire a photographer or videographer to capture all the sweet moments—you’ll want to relive them, and it’s a great way to share your day with loved ones later. Imagine the amazing pictures of you two against a backdrop of Tennessee’s rolling hills!

                  5. Plan a Celebration Later.

                    If you still want to celebrate with friends and family after your elopement, consider hosting a casual gathering or party when you return. This way, you get the best of both worlds—a private, personal wedding day and a chance to celebrate with loved ones afterward.
