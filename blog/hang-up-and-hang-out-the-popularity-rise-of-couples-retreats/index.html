Hang Up and Hang Out: The Popularity Rise of Couples Retreats

Discover what couples retreats have to offer your relationship and learn about The Valley Venues' new cabin retreat option.

Many people long to get away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life, and what better way to do so than by going off the grid? Now is the time to hit pause on life and try your first couples retreat. A couples retreats offers dedicated time away from everyday distractions for partners to focus solely on their relationship.

Retreats may entail a weekend getaway focused on relaxation or lots of activities to strengthen and build your bond. There are five common types of couples retreats:

  • Therapeutic retreats: These are often led by licensed therapists or counselors and are designed to address specific issues within the relationship through individual and joint counseling, workshops, and exercises.
  • Wellness retreats: This type of retreat focuses on holistic healing, with activities that may include yoga, meditation, and spa treatments.
  • Adventure retreats: Get moving! These often include numerous outdoor activities, such as ziplining, hiking, or kayaking. 
  • Spiritual retreats: This type is centered on spiritual growth and connection and often includes prayer, meditation, and mindfulness.
  • Luxury retreats: An upscale getaway where you and your partner are catered to and have access to luxury amenities.

Popularity of Couples Retreats

Couples retreats are becoming increasingly popular because they offer dedicated time and space to slow down together. This time allows couples to focus on each other, whether it’s rekindling a sense of excitement, prioritizing communication, or simply enjoying each other’s presence. Couples retreat attendees reap benefits that a different type of vacation may not offer, such as the following.

A Break from Technology and Work

In today’s digital age, it is easy to become so engrossed in your phone or work that it becomes an extension of your entire personality. Working late hours or balancing opposite working schedules can put a strain on your relationship. Couples retreats enable you to disengage from the demands of everyday life and give undivided attention to your partner. 

In retreat settings, the use of technology is often limited. Studies have shown that technology can put a strain on relationships, leading to dissatisfaction and unhappiness. Stowing your phone and laptop away for a few days can help you form healthy habits that you can bring home and strengthen your relationship in the long term.

Renewing and Reviving Intimacy 

Retreats welcome couples at any stage of their relationship, whether they are newly married or approaching their 50th anniversary. Physical and emotional intimacy builds trust, deepens connections, and fosters a healthy relationship overall. 

Intimacy is an essential aspect of any relationship, as it allows you to learn about your partner in a much deeper, more personal way. Bodies produce a hormone called “oxytocin,” also known as the love hormone, which is released during physical and emotional intimate moments. Retreats can revive or strengthen this level of intimacy between two people.
