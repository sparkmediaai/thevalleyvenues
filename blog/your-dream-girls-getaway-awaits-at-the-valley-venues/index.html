Your Dream Girls’ Getaway Awaits at The Valley Venues

Learn about the growing popularity of girls' getaway and why they are essential, plus how The Valley Venues offers the perfect setting.

A girls’ getaway is more than just a trip—it’s an opportunity to bond, relax and create new memories with your best friends in a beautiful destination. Whether you are planning a bachelorette weekend, celebrating a milestone or simply just craving quality time with your besties, a girls’ vacation is the perfect escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. No matter if you saw your girls last weekend or if it’s been years, a girls’ trip is a great way to strengthen your friendships and reconnect. 

At The Valley Venues, we offer the ideal destination for your next girls’ escape, with a variety of accommodations to suit all group sizes. From spacious lodges to cozy cabins, we have something perfect for everyone.

Why Girls’ Getaways Matter

Inside Travel reports on a OnePoll survey that shows that 76% of women have taken a vacation with their female friends, highlighting the growing popularity of ladies’ holidays and how important it is to find this time together. In today’s fast-paced world, where women are busier than ever, finding time to bond with friends can be challenging. That’s why girls’ trips have become common, as they provide a well-deserved break from the daily grind. They give women a chance to unwind, relax and focus solely on one another. 

In fact, that same survey found that 80% of women leave a girls’ getaway feeling refreshed, and it’s no wonder! Spending time with your closest friends in a distraction-free environment allows you to strengthen your bonds and simply have fun, so why not carve out some time to reconnect with your best friends and indulge in some self-care? 

A Perfect Home Base for Your Escape

Where luxury meets nature sits The Valley Venues, offering a variety of lodging options to suit every group’s needs. For your larger girls’ trips, our four-bedroom, five-bathroom lodge combines antique charm with modern comfort, creating the perfect home base for group catch-ups with picturesque views. You will have ample space for everyone to hang out and make new memories together. 

For a more intimate experience, we recommend one of our charming tiny cabins. These two-bedroom, two-bathroom cabins cultivate a cozy togetherness with plenty of space to gather but also private areas to unwind. Whether you are enjoying your morning coffee on the porch with breathtaking views or curling up with a good book by the fireplace, both lodging options create the perfect atmosphere for a memorable stay.

Fun, Adventure, and Relaxation Await

One of the best parts of a girls’ vacation is the luxury of doing as much or as little as you want. At The Valley Venues, you will find plenty of ways to fill your days with relaxation, adventure or a mix of both:

  • Lounge by the pool: Soak up the sun poolside with your girls with a refreshing cocktail and meaningful conversation.
  • Relax with spa and wellness treatments: Create a DIY spa day in your cozy lodge, treating you and your girls to some R&R.
  • Explore the outdoors: Stroll through the two miles of walking trails to take in the serene property.
  • Venture nearby: Peruse the local shops, try new restaurants, or explore fun spots like Rosie Mae’s Alpaca Farm and Coffee Shop in Wildwood, Georgia, or visit nearby Chattanooga, Tennessee.
  • Enjoy a wine tasting: Venture to a nearby vineyard to try a variety of wines and spend quality time with your friends.

Book Your Stay Today

Now is the perfect time to reconnect with yourself, your girls, and nature at The Valley Venues. Our hidden gem offers everything you need for the ultimate girls’ getaway. Grab your girls and start planning your next escape today. Visit The Valley Venues to book your stay and discover everything our serene property has to offer. We look forward to welcoming you and your best friends to our beautiful property!
