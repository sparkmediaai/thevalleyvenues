Love Is In the Air With 20% off Engagement Photo Shoots This February

Celebrate your love & nuptials with engagement photos at The Valley Venues. Special offer for shoots during the month of February!

Whether it’s an intimate one-on-one experience or all of your loved ones are in attendance, when your partner proposes, it’s always a day to remember. That’s when the glow kicks in—you’re really getting married! But before you start planning the big day, you need to have your engagement photo shoot. It’s the perfect way to celebrate this time in your lives and to help share your news. Enter The Valley Venues! And if you book your engagement photos at our location between February 1 and Valentine’s Day, you will receive an automatic 20% off the total price! Your photographer can then take your photos at any time in 2025.

Why An Engagement Shoot?

Did you know that 79% of all engaged couples take engagement photos, as reported by The Knot 2023 Real Weddings Study? It’s such an important part of the wedding journey. The engagement shoot is often the first time couples will be professionally photographed together. This is not only a great opportunity to capture your love together for the first time but also an excellent practice run to get more comfortable working with your photographer ahead of your big day! Most couples often have the same photographer who captures their engagement shoots for their wedding as well. That way, you can get to know each other ahead of the wedding and ensure you’re on the same page in terms of vision and style.  

Why The Valley Venues?

While The Valley Venues is known for stunning venue options to create the perfect wedding of any bride’s dreams, these same venues are also perfect for bringing your engagement shoot to life! Your options include the following:

  • The Valley features sprawling green space surrounded by beautiful forest.
  • Lookout Deck overlooks the Valley and bridges the gap between our modern world and the beauty of nature. 
  • Magnolia House is a historic house that features stunning 1890s architecture and offers multiple picture-worthy locations to choose from in and of itself.

And as we mentioned above with the photographer, having your engagement photo shoot at a venue you’re considering for the wedding is an ideal practice run!

What’s more, at The Valley Venues, you can even take your engagement photos across our 74-acre property without worry, as we offer a complimentary golf cart to chauffeur you to various locations.

Book Your Engagement Photos During Our February Special!

If you’re still looking for your perfect venue, then let us sweeten the deal further! During the month of February, we are offering 20% off all engagement shoots. To make things easier on you, this discount will be automatically applied to all shoots booked between February 1 and February 14, making that one less thing for you to worry about. Don’t let this special offer pass you by; photos can be taken any time in 2025! 

Here at The Valley Venues, we pride ourselves on providing all of our clients with stress-free events. We take care of all of the planning so you can take care of yourself and enjoy your day!

To learn more about our engagement photo shoots or to view our other services and venues, you can visit our website at https://thevalleyvenues.com/.
