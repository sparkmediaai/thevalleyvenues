Five Reasons to Book Your Summer Wedding at The Valley Venues

Discover why you should book your summer wedding at The Valley Venues in Wildwood, Georgia and learn about their different venue offerings.

Finding your perfect wedding venue can be a long, stressful process, especially with summer wedding season right around the corner. Couples must consider many things when selecting their summer wedding location. Your special day deserves an equally special place, and we have just the solution for you.

At The Valley Venues, we pride ourselves on our selection of curated spaces, excellent customer service and big-picture wedding planning so you never miss a beat on your special day. And it’s all brought together by our visionary founder Kobi Cummings, a Certified Wedding Planner who used her background in design to create a wedding venue like no other.

Explore some of the reasons why The Valley Venues is the ideal spot for your summer wedding. 

1. Elegant Ambiance and Scenery

Located in Wildwood, Georgia, just outside of Chattanooga, TN, couples and guests alike love the picturesque view of the rolling hills and lush greenery. At its peak in the summertime, our surrounding foliage is sure to complete a beautiful wedding in any one of our four on-site venues.

2. Versatile Spaces

Not exactly sure what type of venue you want? Luckily, we have four versatile options to choose from, all with their own unique qualities to elevate your day. 

The Valley

The Valley is our expansive green space surrounded by luxuriant forest and flowers. Its warm ambiance on a summer night is sure to bring magic to any wedding. We recommend it for ceremonies, receptions, and cocktail hours. 

Lookout Deck

The Lookout Deck oversees the gorgeous mountain views on our property. Equipped with modern provisions and ambient lighting, the Lookout Deck is perfect for all stages of your wedding. 

Magnolia House

Our Magnolia House is truly what sets us apart from other venues. Built in 1890, this restored hallmark home creates a movie-like atmosphere ideal for rehearsal dinners, ceremonies, receptions and cocktail hours.

Davis Hall

Davis Hall is our grand reception hall with a state-of-the-art sound and lighting system and attached food staging area. Large enough to accommodate up to 250 attendees, Davis Hall is a timeless pick for a wedding venue. 

3. Stress-free Planning

When you have your wedding at The Valley Venues, we are here to help you every step of the way. From personalizing your vision for your ceremony to assisting you during photos, our team is dedicated to giving you unparalleled support. Our expertise in design and planning is here to assist you in formulating a flawless day. 

4. Lodging

Do you prefer your wedding party to stay onsite at the venue? The Valley Venue’s stunning 4-bedroom, 5-bath lodge blends antique charm with modern comfort, providing a spacious retreat with breathtaking views. It’s the perfect home base for convenience, relaxation and connection during your summer wedding.

For a more intimate experience, The Valley Venues also offers four charming tiny cabins:

  • One 2-bedroom, 2-bath cabin – great for small families or friends.
  • Three 1-bedroom, 1-bath cabins – ideal for couples or solo travelers.

5. In-House Catering

Unlike some other venues, we are happy to provide in-house catering and bar services for our weddings and events. We offer over 30 signature hors d’oeuvres and a variety of dinner catering packages. Our catering services are not a requirement when booking with us, but we are proud to serve various dietary needs, along with a large selection of desserts. What’s more, if you crave something you don’t see on our menu, please let us know, as our Chef is happy to create custom dishes!

We understand that planning your special day is no easy feat, and we are committed to giving you the best summer wedding experience imaginable. And the reasons above are just the start! If you’re interested in seeing how we can bring your summer wedding dream to life, please contact us today!
