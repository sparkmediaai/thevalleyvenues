It was clear that everyone at The Valley Venues is dedicated to creating an unforgettable experience.
  • Our outdoor ceremony and reception were exactly what we had envisioned—perfectly framed by the stunning mountain backdrop and vibrant fall foliage. The scenery was breathtaking, and the weather couldn’t have been more ideal.
  • Overall, our wedding weekend was everything we hoped for and more. We will always cherish the memories of this beautiful place, the incredible views, and the warm hospitality we received. We are beyond grateful to Kobi and the entire team for making our day so special.
  • What a wedding!! Kobi welcomed us with less than 2 weeks notice for a 225 person wedding when we unexpectedly found out that our original hotel couldn’t handle our event. She met us over Labor Day weekend so we could tour the property and talk about the logistics. Kobi was kind, calming, and sympathetic to the situation the hotel had put us in. Valley Venues is a beautiful space and the outdoor views are breathtaking! The outdoor cocktail hour was perfect for my daughter’s “garden party” theme.
  • The deck/patio had plenty of space for 11 bistro tables, a bar, Clumpies ice cream cart and a lot of room for our guests to stroll. The Lodge was a beautiful space for the bride and her bridal party to stay the night before. The groom and groomsmen arrived the next day and spent the day in their space playing pool and relaxing. Our guests stayed at the Double Tree in downtown Chattanooga (also a wonderful vendor as they accommodated our out of town guests on short notice), and it was a short and easy drive to Valley Venues. If you want a wedding in Chattanooga, make an appointment to tour The Valley Venues!!

Kobi and all her staff were amazing to work with in preparation and delivery of everything we needed to make the weekend a treasured memory for all who attended.
